Why this place has a name

I'm spending my career building spaces that make design inclusive rather than exclusive, and imagining cities that live closer to nature, with equal access to design, art and culture.

I named my home The House of the People because I hope it becomes a place where artists, designers and creators can stay for a while and feel at home.

You're one of the first. I expect this idea to keep growing and, one day, to move to a much bigger home: a house that is also a jungle, built for living in real connection with nature.

Make yourself at home. — Edu

Heat and AC

The panel is in a mid-height square on the right side of the library, behind the couch. The top button on the left is MODE, which switches between heat and AC. The two buttons below it set the temperature: the upper one is warmer, the lower one cooler. Please leave the other buttons alone, since they change the schedule.

Once it's below 60°F outside, keep it on heat and never switch it off. At home, choose whatever feels good. When you go out or travel, keep it at 64°F or higher so the plants stay happy and the pipes can't freeze.

Playing music

The KEF LS50 Wireless II speakers are already on the home wifi. Join the wifi first, then:

  • From an iPhone or Mac, choose the KEF speakers in AirPlay.
  • From Android or Chrome, cast to them with Chromecast.
  • In Spotify, pick them from the devices menu.
  • For Tidal, Qobuz, Amazon Music or radio, use the free KEF Connect app.
  • Bluetooth works too.

They're powerful speakers, so please keep the volume kind to the neighbors, especially at night.

The plants

Wednesday is plant day. Feel the soil and water only the ones that are ready. When in doubt, wait a few days.

Variegated fishbone cactus

First check Oct 21

When the mix is dry well past the top inch. Be stingy, it's recovering.

Bright indirect light, no direct sun. Keep near the humidifier.

Green fishbone cactus

First check Oct 21

When the top 1–2″ is dry. About every 10–14 days in winter.

Bright indirect light. Keep near the humidifier.

Monstera

First check Oct 21

When the top 2–3″ is dry, then water thoroughly. About every 10–14 days in winter.

Bright indirect light. Tuck aerial roots onto the moss pole.

Rubber plant

First check Oct 21

When the top 1–2″ is dry. About every 7–10 days; it likes the drier side.

Bright light. Wipe the glossy leaves now and then.

Ponytail palm

First check Oct 28

When the soil is completely dry, then water deeply. Every 3–4 weeks in winter.

As much sun as possible. It stores water in its trunk.

Pencil cactus

First check Oct 28

When the soil is completely dry. Every 2–3 weeks, less in winter.

Milky sap is toxic. Wear gloves if trimming and wash your hands.

Good habits for all of them

  • Always check the soil with a finger before watering. These are ranges, not a calendar.
  • Once a month, wipe dusty leaves and glance under them for tiny pests.
  • Give pots a quarter-turn every few weeks so they grow evenly.
  • No fertilizer over the winter.
